# FinOps executive deck

Use this skill to turn verified findings into a short executive PowerPoint deck.

## Required content

Show the assessment scope, access boundary, evidence, limitations, and next decision. Keep different units separate. Label synthetic data as synthetic. The deck must not state an estimated saving unless a finding contains the documented formula and assumptions.

## Quality gate

Generate the deck from structured findings, validate its slide count and text layout, render it for visual review, and keep the source evidence available in the repository. Do not use screenshots as a substitute for editable evidence.
